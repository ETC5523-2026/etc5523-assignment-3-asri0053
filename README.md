## Blog post

**Not All Hospital Infections Have the Same Impact**

This post explores whether the most common healthcare-associated infections also cause the greatest health loss. It compares estimated case numbers and healthy years lost across five infection types in Germany in 2011.

The main message is that infection frequency and health consequences provide different perspectives, and both matter when interpreting public health statistics.

## Intended audience

The post is written for general readers, including university students interested in public health, who have no specialist medical or statistical knowledge.

It introduces technical terms in plain language and uses a chart and table to explain the findings, alongside uncertainty and limitations.
